# Derivation ledger: caustests::xtpcaus(test = "pfty") against Dumitrescu and Hurlin (2012)

Package: caustests 1.1.7, file R/xtpcaus.R (line numbers refer to that version). Paper: Dumitrescu and Hurlin (2012), Economic Modelling 29(4), 1450-1460, doi 10.1016/j.econmod.2012.02.014. Audit of version 1.1.6: /audit/caustests/report.md and ledger_xtpcaus.md (Stage 1 and 2); this file is the Stage 3 update.

Scope note. xtpcaus(test = "pfty") is a Panel Fourier Toda-Yamamoto test (Yilanci and Gorus 2020; Fisher combination of Emirmahmutoglu and Kose 2011). Since 1.1.7 the plain specification kmax = 0, dmax = 0 is the Dumitrescu and Hurlin (DH) regression of eq. (1) with a unit-specific lag order K_i = p_i, and the DH standardised statistics are reported for that specification only. For the Fourier or Toda-Yamamoto augmented specification the DH statistics are NA.

Verification in tests/testthat/test-xtpcaus-dh.R (tolerance 1e-10 against lm()): W_i (both variance normalisations), selected p_i and f_i, residual df, W_bar, Z_bar, Z_tilde (eq. 25 and eq. 26 forms), one-sided p-values, Fisher statistic, df guard, NA error, misaligned panel error, singular-unit warning, RNG scope.
